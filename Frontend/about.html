SUPPORT

Frequently Asked Questions

We pride ourselves on a hybrid sourcing model. We partner with highly skilled local artisans across Cameroon for unique, handcrafted pieces, and we also collaborate with international manufacturers known for their premium materials and advanced techniques, ensuring a diverse and high-quality inventory.

We offer white-glove delivery service for all large furniture items within major cities, which includes placement in your room of choice and removal of packaging. For smaller items and nationwide delivery, we use trusted courier partners. Delivery times and costs are calculated at checkout based on your location and item size.

We stand by the quality of our products. If you are not completely satisfied, we accept returns on standard items within 14 days of delivery, provided they are in their original condition. Custom orders are non-refundable. Please review our full return policy page for detailed instructions.

Our flagship showroom is located in the commercial center of Douala. We also have a dedicated boutique store in Yaoundé. Please visit our Contact Us page for exact addresses, operating hours, and map directions to experience our collections in person.
